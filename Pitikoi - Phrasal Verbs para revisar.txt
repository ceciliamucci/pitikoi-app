VOCABULARIO COMPARTIDO

GET UP
Meaning: to get out of bed
Español: levantarse
Example: I woke up at seven, but I didn't get up until eight.
Pitikoi Tip: Wake up means stop sleeping. Get up means leave your bed.

WAKE UP
Meaning: to stop sleeping, or make someone stop sleeping
Español: despertarse / despertar a alguien
Example: Don't wake me up. I've got the day off.
Pitikoi Tip: With a pronoun, say wake me up, not wake up me.

GET IN
Meaning: to enter a car or taxi
Español: subir a un auto o taxi
Example: Get in the car. I'll drive.
Pitikoi Tip: Usually get in a car or taxi, but get on a bus, train or plane. To leave: get out of the car / get off the bus.

GET OUT
Meaning: to leave an enclosed place or a car
Español: salir / bajar de un auto
Example: Wait here. I'll get out of the car and check the address.
Pitikoi Tip: Use get out of when naming the place: get out of the car. For a bus, train or plane, use get off.

GET ON
Meaning: to board a bus, train or plane
Español: subir a un colectivo, tren o avión
Example: Let's get on the bus before it fills up.
Pitikoi Tip: Get on the bus, but get in the taxi. Get on also has other meanings, such as having a good relationship: We get on well.

GET OFF
Meaning: to leave a bus, train or plane
Español: bajar de un colectivo, tren o avión
Example: We need to get off at the next stop.
Pitikoi Tip: Get on / get off go together. For cars, use get in / get out of.

GO OUT
Meaning: to leave home for a social activity
Español: salir
Example: Do you want to go out for dinner, or stay in?
Pitikoi Tip: Go out with someone can also mean date them: Are you two going out?

COME BACK
Meaning: to return to where the speaker is, or the place being treated as here
Español: volver / regresar acá
Example: Come back to my place after work. I'll make dinner.
Pitikoi Tip: Come back brings someone toward here. Go back usually means return somewhere else.

GO BACK
Meaning: to return to another place
Español: volver / regresar
Example: I left my keys at the office. I'll have to go back.
Pitikoi Tip: If you're at home, you go back to the office. Someone waiting at the office might say come back.

FIND OUT
Meaning: to discover information
Español: enterarse / averiguar
Example: I just found out the gig's been cancelled.
Pitikoi Tip: Find out means discover information. Figure out means understand or solve something by thinking.

FIGURE OUT
Meaning: to understand or solve something by thinking
Español: entender / resolver
Example: I can't figure out how this thing works.
Pitikoi Tip: You find out a fact, but figure out a problem. With it, say figure it out.

LOOK FOR
Meaning: to try to find someone or something
Español: buscar
Example: Have you seen my keys? I've been looking for them everywhere.
Pitikoi Tip: Look for is the search. Find is the result. Look up means search for information in a reference source.

LOOK AFTER
Meaning: to take care of someone or something
Español: cuidar
Example: Could you look after my cat this weekend?
Pitikoi Tip: Keep the words together: look after her, not look her after.

LOOK UP
Meaning: to find information in a reference source
Español: consultar / buscar información
Example: I'm not sure what that word means. Let's look it up.
Pitikoi Tip: Look up a word / look a word up. With a pronoun, always look it up.

PICK UP
Meaning: to collect someone or something
Español: pasar a buscar / recoger
Example: I'll pick you up outside the station at eight.
Meaning: to lift something
Español: levantar algo
Example: Can you pick that bag up? It's blocking the door.
Pitikoi Tip: Pick you up, not pick up you. Drop someone off means take them somewhere and leave them there.

DROP OFF
Meaning: to take someone or something somewhere and leave them there
Español: llevar y dejar
Example: Can you drop me off at the corner?
Pitikoi Tip: Pick me up means collect me. Drop me off means leave me at my destination.

PUT ON
Meaning: to start wearing something
Español: ponerse ropa o accesorios
Example: Put your jacket on. It's freezing out there.
Pitikoi Tip: Put on is the action. Wear describes having the clothes on. With it: put it on.

TAKE OFF
Meaning: to remove clothing
Español: quitarse ropa
Example: You can take your shoes off by the door.
Meaning: for an aircraft to leave the ground
Español: despegar
Example: Our plane took off an hour late.
Pitikoi Tip: Context matters: take off your coat means remove it; a plane takes off when it leaves the ground.

TURN ON
Meaning: to make a device start working
Español: encender
Example: Can you turn the heating on? I'm cold.
Pitikoi Tip: Turn it on, not turn on it. The opposite is turn off.

TURN OFF
Meaning: to make a device stop working
Español: apagar
Example: Did you turn the oven off before we left?
Pitikoi Tip: Turn off is an action someone takes. Break down usually means a machine stops working because of a problem.

SET UP
Meaning: to prepare or arrange something so it is ready to use
Español: configurar / preparar / organizar
Example: Give me a minute to set up the video call.
Pitikoi Tip: Set up can describe equipment, an account or a meeting. With it: set it up.

WORK OUT
Meaning: to exercise
Español: hacer ejercicio
Example: I usually work out before breakfast.
Meaning: to find a solution or calculate something
Español: resolver / calcular
Example: Let's work out how much we each owe.
Meaning: to develop successfully
Español: salir bien / funcionar
Example: I hope things work out with your new roommate.
Pitikoi Tip: Work out can mean exercise, solve something or turn out well. Listen to the rest of the sentence.

GIVE UP
Meaning: to stop trying or stop a habit
Español: rendirse / dejar un hábito
Example: Don't give up. You're nearly there.
Pitikoi Tip: Use a noun or -ing form: give up coffee / give up smoking.

KEEP UP
Meaning: to stay at the same pace or level
Español: seguir el ritmo / mantenerse al día
Example: Slow down! I can't keep up with you.
Pitikoi Tip: Use keep up with someone or something: keep up with the class / keep up with the news.

RUN OUT OF
Meaning: to have no more of something left
Español: quedarse sin
Example: We've run out of milk. Want me to get some?
Pitikoi Tip: Name the missing thing after of: run out of time, money or milk.

HANG OUT
Meaning: to spend time relaxing with someone or somewhere
Español: pasar el rato / juntarse
Example: Want to hang out at my place later?
Pitikoi Tip: Hang out with friends describes relaxed time together, not a formal appointment.

SHOW UP
Meaning: to arrive or appear
Español: aparecer / llegar
Example: We waited for half an hour, but he never showed up.
Pitikoi Tip: Show up often comes up when someone is expected, late or a surprise.

END UP
Meaning: to finally be in a situation, often an unexpected one
Español: terminar / acabar haciendo algo
Example: We went for one coffee and ended up talking all afternoon.
Pitikoi Tip: Use end up + -ing: end up staying. You can also end up somewhere: We ended up at her place.

BRING UP
Meaning: to start talking about a subject
Español: sacar un tema / mencionar
Example: Please don't bring up work at dinner.
Pitikoi Tip: Bring it up, not bring up it. Bring up can also mean raise a child, but here it means mention a topic.

COME UP WITH
Meaning: to think of an idea or solution
Español: idear / ocurrírsele algo a alguien
Example: We need to come up with a cheaper plan.
Pitikoi Tip: Come up with introduces a new idea. Find out discovers information that already exists.

POINT OUT
Meaning: to draw attention to a fact or detail
Español: señalar / hacer notar
Example: Thanks for pointing out the typo. I hadn't noticed it.
Pitikoi Tip: Point out a mistake means make someone aware of it, not necessarily fix it.

DEAL WITH
Meaning: to handle a situation, problem or person
Español: ocuparse de / lidiar con
Example: I'll deal with the complaint. You finish your lunch.
Pitikoi Tip: Deal with focuses on handling something. Put up with focuses on tolerating something unpleasant. They can overlap, so context matters.

CALM DOWN
Meaning: to become less upset or excited, or help someone do so
Español: calmarse / tranquilizarse / calmar a alguien
Example: Let's take a walk and calm down before we talk about it.
Pitikoi Tip: You calm down, or calm someone down. With a pronoun: calm her down.

BREAK DOWN
Meaning: to stop working because of a fault
Español: averiarse / dejar de funcionar
Example: My car broke down on the way to work.
Meaning: to lose emotional control
Español: quebrarse emocionalmente
Example: She broke down crying when she heard the news.
Meaning: to divide or explain something in simpler parts
Español: desglosar / explicar por partes
Example: That's a lot to take in. Can you break it down for me?
Pitikoi Tip: The context determines the meaning: a broken machine, a strong emotion or a step-by-step explanation.

PUT UP WITH
Meaning: to tolerate something unpleasant
Español: soportar / aguantar
Example: I can't put up with this noise anymore.
Pitikoi Tip: Put up with emphasizes tolerating an annoyance. Deal with can mean actively handling a problem. Keep the phrase together: put up with it.
